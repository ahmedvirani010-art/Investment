- Well-established brand with 75+ years of history

### Why High D/E is Normal for Banks
The Debt/Equity ratio of 4.80 might seem concerning for a regular company, but for banks:
- **Leverage is the business model** - banks borrow (deposits) to lend
- Deposits are classified as liabilities/debt on balance sheet
- Regulatory capital adequacy ratios are more important than D/E
- **Focus on ROE, asset quality, and capital adequacy instead**

### Banking Metrics Priority
For banks, prioritize these metrics:
1. **ROE** - Efficiency of equity deployment (HBL: 18.5% ✅)
2. **Asset Quality** - NPL ratio and provisioning coverage
3. **Net Interest Margin** - Spread between lending and borrowing rates
4. **Cost-to-Income Ratio** - Operational efficiency
5. **Capital Adequacy** - Regulatory compliance and buffer
